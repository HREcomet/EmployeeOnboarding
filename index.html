The ECOMET Standard

Leadership Principles

Operating standards that define how we make decisions, interact with customers, and run the business every day.

01

Customer First

Focus on customer outcomes above all else—faster delivery, higher reliability, lower cost, and full transparency. Prioritize customer impact over internal convenience.

02

End-to-End Ownership

Take full accountability for outcomes. When problems arise, own them end-to-end until fully resolved across sales, ops, procurement, and technology.

03

Bias for Execution

Prioritize speed of execution over extended deliberation. Treat execution speed as a core competitive advantage by deciding, assigning, and executing swiftly.

04

Data-Driven Decisions

Rely on data to run the business—cost, SLA performance, utilization, turnaround time, and network efficiency. Separate facts from assumptions.

05

Raise the Bar

Continuously raise performance standards across cost, speed, reliability, and customer experience. Treat today's best performance as tomorrow's baseline.

06

Frugality

Treat every rupee, vehicle, and minute as a critical resource. Eliminate waste, maximize capacity utilization, and achieve more output with less waste.

07

Build Trust

Build trust through consistent execution and transparent communication. Make commitments carefully, communicate risks early, and take ownership when things go wrong.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for getting started at ECOMET.

How soon should I submit my onboarding form?

Please submit your onboarding form on Day 1 so that workspace credentials, system privileges, and team access can be configured without delay.

When are the daily and weekly business strategy standups?

Daily Business Standups take place every weekday morning at 9:00 AM EST. The Weekly Business Strategy Standup occurs every Monday morning at 10:00 AM EST.

Who do I contact for IT or system access issues?

For all IT, access credentials, and HR inquiries, please email HR@ecometlogistics.com.
